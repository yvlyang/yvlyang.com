bhop_brick

A bunnyhopping game you can play in your browser.

Play now →

Desktop · keyboard & mouse required

Why I made this

Bunnyhopping is one of my favorite and most useless hobbies. I started when I was 12, and over the years, I’ve somehow gotten pretty good at it.

It’s not supposed to be a serious competitive game. It’s mostly an excuse to combine programming with a completely unnecessary skill I’ve somehow been practicing for years.

How to play

Bunnyhopping is hard. Getting your mouse turns and movement keys in sync takes practice, so don’t worry if you keep falling at first. Start slow and focus on landing a few jumps in a row.

Start with Autohop. Use W to get moving and hold Space to jump. In the air, release W and hold A while turning left, or D while turning right. Keep hopping to the finish.

R to restart · Esc to release your mouse.
